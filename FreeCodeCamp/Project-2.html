About Japan

Discover Japan by experiencing the blend of ancient tradition and modern innovation: witness Mount Fuji, explore Kyoto’s temples and Kyoto Arashiyama Bamboo Grove, indulge in sushi at local markets, and experience Tokyo’s vibrant nightlife. Key highlights include hot springs (onsens), bullet trains, anime culture, and seasonal beauty like cherry blossoms.
